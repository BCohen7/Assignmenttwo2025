Domestic Air Travel in Australia

As a result of our remoteness, large portions of Australia are depended on flight to travel for both leisure and work. Australian Domestic Aviation Operators carried over 5 million passengers in August of this year alone, with almost 60 million passengers being carried in 2024. While Qantas is known as the national carrier of Australia, competition is still strong within the sector, which this visualisation highlights.

Due to the long distances Australians cover, knowing that a carrier is likely to get you to your destination as often as possible is key. While some budget airlines may carry reputations for poor services offered, the heatmap below shows that this quite often is not the case. Apart from Bonza's demise early in 2024, budget considerate carriers, such as Jetstar and Virgin typically perform very reliaby.
